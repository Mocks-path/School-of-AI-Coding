Read the response — find the answer in the JSON

When the API answers, it sends back JSON — labeled boxes of data. The reply text is in there, next to useful extras like which model answered and how many tokens you were billed. Learn where each piece lives and you can pull out exactly what you need. Click a field, then click what it holds.

Cost reality check — tokens add up

You pay per token — a tiny chunk of text, roughly three-quarters of a word. You're billed for both what you send in (input) and what comes back (output). More words in, and longer answers out, means more cost. Move the sliders to feel how it scales.

💡 Honest note: these numbers are illustrative only — real prices vary a lot by provider and model and change over time (this uses a sample rate of about $0.002 per 1,000 tokens). The durable lesson isn't the exact figure; it's the direction: fewer and shorter tokens cost less. Trim your prompts and cap the reply length, and your bill follows.
